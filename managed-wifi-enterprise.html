Pricing & Contract Terms

  • Prices are monthly rentals and apply to the specified access-point quantity ranges. They are not per-access-point prices.
  • The initial contract term is three years (36 months).
  • After the initial three-year term, the rental continues unless the agreement is terminated or changed in accordance with the contract.
  • Following the initial three-year term, the customer may request a device upgrade at the same monthly rental, subject to technical compatibility, device availability, site requirements, and the agreed contractual terms.
  • Final eligibility, installation scope, hardware specifications, VAT treatment, and any additional charges are confirmed in the formal quotation. Prices do not necessarily include every installation, cabling, internet subscription or hardware requirement.

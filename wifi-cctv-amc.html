AMC Service Areas for Existing Wi-Fi & CCTV

  • Preventive maintenance visits according to the agreed contract
  • Wireless access-point health checks
  • Connectivity and performance troubleshooting
  • Switch and network configuration checks
  • CCTV camera connectivity checks
  • NVR/DVR connectivity and recording checks where access and compatibility permit
  • Fault diagnosis and escalation
  • Configuration backup where supported
  • Maintenance reports
  • Recommendations for network upgrades
  • Remote support where technically available
  • Optional on-site technical support
